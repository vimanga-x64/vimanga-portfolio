02 / Research

Questions worth staying with.

Research across adaptive learning, graph neural networks, health AI and language technology.

  1. 01 Graph learning · 2026

    Top-k Densest Subgraph Augmentation for Hypergraph Neural Networks

    Density-based augmentation can strengthen natural clusters, but may erase useful sparse signals. Gains of up to 4% on movie-profit prediction and very different outcomes on benchmark networks.

     Izmailov, Gowthaman, Shirodkar & Umange ↗
  2. 02 Natural language processing · 2026

    Transformer-Based Automated Interlinear Glossing for Low-Resource Languages

    A character-level Transformer combining unsupervised morpheme segmentation, relative positional encoding and translation cues.

     Kaceli & Umange ↗
  3. 03 Adaptive learning · Master’s thesis

    Hybrid POMDP for Mobile Intelligent Tutoring Systems

    A Deep Q-Network controller jointly selects pedagogical actions, compute placement and avatar fidelity. 44% relative improvement in normalized learning gain; 98.68% of steps under 500 ms.

     Umange ↗
  4. 04 Health AI · 2025

    Comparing Machine Learning Approaches for Heart Disease Indicators

    From logistic regression to TabNet: which models most reliably detect heart-disease indicators, and how systematic tuning changes the answer.

     Umange & Buch ↗
